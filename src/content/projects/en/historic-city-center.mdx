Toledo's historic center, a World Heritage Site, required a solution to help tourists navigate its labyrinthine streets while discovering its rich multicultural history. We developed a digital experience that combines artificial intelligence, 3D maps, and deep cultural content.

Built with progressive web technology and custom Mapbox maps, the system includes a conversational AI assistant trained with local historians to answer questions in real-time.

Key project elements:
- Multilingual conversational AI assistant
- 3D maps of historic center with orientation
- Thematic routes (Jewish, Christian, Muslim, artistic)
- Historical recreations with augmented reality
- Integration with local shops and restaurants
- Personalized recommendation system
- 360° spatial audio at key points
- Night mode with special lighting

The platform has received international recognition, winning the "Best Digital Tourism Innovation 2024" award. It attracts 50,000 monthly visitors and has helped decongest main routes by distributing tourists more evenly throughout the historic center.
