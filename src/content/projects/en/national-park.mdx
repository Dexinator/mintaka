Ordesa and Monte Perdido National Park needed a solution to educate visitors about its unique biodiversity while promoting sustainable tourism practices. We created a nature-specialized audio guide that works completely offline.

Developed with native technology and optimized for prolonged use without connection, it includes a complete database of the park's flora, fauna, and geology. The minimalist design ensures maximum readability under direct sunlight.

Main features:
- AI-powered visual identifier for plants and animals
- Hiking routes with elevation profiles
- Safety and weather alerts
- Educational content about Pyrenean ecosystems
- Wildlife sighting log for conservation
- Integrated "Leave No Trace" principles
- Visit environmental impact calculator
- SOS with geolocation for emergencies

The project won the "Green Tourism Award" for its focus on sustainability. Surveys show that 92% of users adopted more responsible behaviors after using the app. The park reports a 35% reduction in incidents due to inappropriate behavior.
