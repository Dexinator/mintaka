The National Archaeological Museum sought to modernize its visitor experience without losing its educational and cultural essence. We developed a complete digital audio guide that combines professional narration, augmented reality, and interactive multimedia content.

The project was developed with PWA technology to ensure offline functionality and React Native for native experience on iOS and Android. The design prioritizes universal accessibility with support for multiple levels of detail based on visitor interest.

Main project features:
- 3D reconstructions of archaeological pieces
- Narration in 8 languages with professional actors
- Interactive maps with indoor GPS navigation
- Children's mode with educational gamification
- Personal favorites and notes system
- Integration with museum ticketing system
- Downloadable content for complete offline use

The result is an experience that has increased average visit time by 45%, and visitor satisfaction reached 4.8/5 stars. The museum reports that 78% of visitors now use the digital audio guide.
