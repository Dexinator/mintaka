The Camino de Santiago is a millennial experience that needed to adapt to 21st-century pilgrims. We created a comprehensive application that guides walkers through the French route, providing historical, cultural, and practical information in real-time.

The application was developed with Flutter for maximum performance on both mobile platforms, with special attention to battery optimization for long journeys. The Node.js backend manages real-time updates on hostels, weather, and alerts.

Featured functionalities:
- GPS with route tracking and deviation alerts
- Geolocated historical information for each town
- Updated hostel listing with availability
- Pilgrim community with chat and meetups
- Digital diary with geotagged photos and reflections
- Arrival time prediction based on pace
- Digital pilgrim credential management
- Exclusive podcasts about Camino history

More than 35,000 pilgrims have used the app, with a 4.9/5 rating. The cultural content has been praised for its depth and accessibility, making the Camino more meaningful for visitors from all over the world.
